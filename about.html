Everything for long threads.

长对话需要的能力都在这里。

Designed around the real friction of reading, revisiting, and continuing long ChatGPT conversations.

围绕长对话里的阅读、回看、定位和继续处理体验设计。

1

Side navigator

侧边导航

Turn every conversation into a vertical map of clickable message nodes.

把整段对话变成一条可点击的消息地图。

2

Hover preview

悬浮预览

Preview message content before jumping, so you keep your place.

跳转前先预览消息内容，减少来回滚动。

3

Role filters

角色筛选

Switch between All, AI, and Me to find the exact part of a thread.

在全部、AI、我的输入之间切换，快速找到目标段落。

4

AI reply folding

AI 回复折叠

Collapse long AI replies with independent expand and collapse controls.

自动折叠较长 AI 回复，并为每条回复提供独立展开和收起。

5

Latest-message positioning

自动定位最新消息

Open an existing conversation and land near the newest message automatically.

打开已有对话时自动定位到最新消息附近。

6

Clean controls

清爽控制

Hide overlapping native controls and tune the side offset for your layout.

可隐藏干扰控件，并调整侧边距离适配自己的界面。

Local by design.

默认本地运行。

ThreadPilot only stores extension preferences in your browser. It does not upload conversations, run analytics, or inject ads.

ThreadPilot 只在浏览器本地保存扩展偏好，不上传对话内容，不做分析追踪，也不插入广告。
