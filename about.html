What it does

它能做什么

Adds message navigation, hover previews, role filters, and long AI reply folding.

提供消息导航、悬浮预览、角色筛选和 AI 长回复折叠。

2

Privacy

隐私

Your conversations stay in your browser. No analytics, ads, or conversation upload.

对话内容保留在浏览器中，不做分析追踪，不插入广告，也不上传对话。

3

Settings

设置

Preferences such as visibility, side, filter, and folding height are saved locally.

导航显示、位置、筛选和折叠高度等偏好会保存在本地。
